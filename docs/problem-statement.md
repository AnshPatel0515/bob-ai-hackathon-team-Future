# Problem Statement

Supply Chain Disruption Assistant & Fleet Utilisation Optimizer ■ Ongoing Pain Supply chain disruptions — weather events, port strikes, geopolitical crises — cascade across hundreds of active shipments in ways that are impossible to track manually. Fleet assets (trucks, containers, vessels) sit idle while other routes are overloaded. Cold chain shipments (vaccines, perishables) are especially vulnerable — a single temperature excursion across any leg can spoil a $500K+ cargo, but breaches are only discovered at delivery when it is too late.

## Background

Supply chain transportation is a critical part of modern business operations, connecting suppliers, warehouses, ports, distributors, and customers. The timely movement of goods depends on multiple factors such as traffic conditions, weather, port congestion, vehicle availability, route conditions, and unexpected disruptions.

Transportation disruptions can cause shipment delays, increased operational costs, missed delivery deadlines, and customer dissatisfaction. Logistics managers often need to make quick decisions using information from multiple sources, making it difficult to identify risks and select the best alternative route or action.

This project addresses these challenges by providing a centralized transportation management solution that combines shipment data with external disruption information. The system uses AI-assisted analysis to identify potential transportation risks, predict disruptions, suggest optimized routes or actions, and provide timely alerts to logistics managers.

The goal is to improve supply chain visibility, support faster decision-making, reduce the impact of transportation disruptions, and improve the reliability and efficiency of deliveries.

## The Problem

Logistics managers often have to make transportation decisions while dealing with incomplete and constantly changing information. Shipment status, traffic conditions, weather disruptions, port congestion, vehicle availability, and route conditions may come from different sources, making it difficult to identify risks early.

A single disruption can create a chain reaction across the supply network, causing delivery delays, higher transportation costs, missed deadlines, and customer dissatisfaction. In many cases, logistics teams react to disruptions only after they have already affected a shipment.

The core problem is the lack of a centralized, intelligent system that can combine transportation data with external disruption signals, identify high-risk shipments or routes, predict potential delays, and recommend appropriate actions in time for logistics managers to respond.

Our project addresses this problem by providing an AI-assisted transportation management solution that helps logistics managers monitor operations, detect disruptions early, assess transportation risk, and make faster, data-driven decisions.


## Who is Affected

The primary users affected by this problem are **logistics managers and supply chain operations teams** responsible for coordinating shipments, monitoring transportation routes, and ensuring on-time delivery.

* **Logistics Managers:** Monitor shipments and routes, identify transportation risks, and make decisions when disruptions occur.
* **Supply Chain Operations Teams:** Coordinate vehicles, warehouses, suppliers, and deliveries while managing unexpected transportation issues.
* **Fleet and Transportation Coordinators:** Track vehicle availability, routes, delivery schedules, and potential delays.
* **Business and Operations Managers:** Are affected by increased transportation costs, delayed deliveries, and disruptions across the supply chain.

The problem is particularly significant for organizations managing **multiple shipments and transportation routes simultaneously**, where manually monitoring changing conditions and responding to disruptions becomes difficult and time-consuming.


## Why It Matters

Transportation disruptions can have a significant impact on supply chain operations. Even a single unexpected delay can affect multiple downstream shipments, increasing transportation costs and reducing customer satisfaction.

* **Higher operational costs:** Delays can increase fuel consumption, vehicle utilization, storage costs, and emergency transportation expenses.
* **Delivery delays:** Disruptions can cause shipments to miss planned delivery windows and affect downstream operations.
* **Revenue impact:** Late or failed deliveries can result in lost sales, penalties, cancelled orders, and reduced customer trust.
* **Decision-making pressure:** Logistics managers must often make time-sensitive decisions with incomplete or changing information.
* **Limited visibility:** When transportation, weather, traffic, and port information is scattered across different sources, identifying risks early becomes difficult.
* **Cascading disruptions:** A delay in one route or shipment can create a chain reaction across warehouses, suppliers, vehicles, and customers.

By detecting potential disruptions earlier and providing AI-assisted risk analysis and recommendations, the proposed solution can help logistics teams respond faster, reduce avoidable delays, improve route planning, and increase overall supply chain resilience.

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Many logistics teams currently rely on a combination of spreadsheets, transportation management systems, GPS tracking tools, weather services, traffic information, and manual communication to monitor shipments and respond to disruptions. While these tools provide useful information, they often operate separately and do not provide a unified view of transportation risks.

Existing solutions also tend to focus on **tracking and reporting after an event has occurred**, rather than proactively predicting potential disruptions and recommending the best response. Logistics managers may need to manually compare multiple data sources before deciding whether to change a route, adjust a delivery schedule, or take other corrective action.

This approach becomes increasingly difficult when managing multiple shipments and routes at the same time. The lack of centralized, AI-assisted risk analysis can lead to slower decisions, missed warning signals, unnecessary costs, and avoidable delivery delays.

Our solution addresses this gap by combining transportation data with external disruption signals and AI-powered analysis in a single platform. It helps logistics managers move from **reactive monitoring to proactive disruption management** by identifying risks, predicting potential impacts, and providing actionable recommendations.
