## 🎯 Problem Statement

> In 2–3 sentences: What problem does your project solve? Who experiences this problem?

Supply chain managers and logistics teams struggle to track how weather events, port strikes, geopolitical crises, and route disruptions affect hundreds of shipments at the same time. This can cause shipment delays, underutilized or overloaded fleet assets, and serious risks for cold-chain cargo such as vaccines and perishables, where a temperature excursion can lead to major financial losses.

---

## 💡 Solution

> In 2–3 sentences: What did you build? How does it solve the problem above?

We built an AI-powered Supply Chain Disruption Assistant and Fleet Utilisation Optimizer that monitors shipments, fleet assets, disruptions, and cold-chain temperatures in one platform. It identifies high-risk shipments, detects temperature breaches and disruption impacts, and recommends better fleet allocation so logistics teams can respond faster, reduce delays, protect valuable cargo, and improve fleet utilization.

---

## ✨ Key Features=

* **Feature 1: Real-Time Disruption Monitoring** — Tracks supply chain disruptions such as weather events, port strikes, and geopolitical issues and identifies potentially affected shipments.

* **Feature 2: Cold-Chain Risk Detection** — Monitors shipment temperature data and instantly flags temperature excursions that could damage vaccines, pharmaceuticals, and perishable cargo.

* **Feature 3: AI-Powered Risk Scoring** — Calculates shipment risk based on disruption severity, cargo type, temperature conditions, and cargo value to help teams prioritize critical shipments.

* **Feature 4: Fleet Utilisation Optimizer** — Tracks vehicle availability, capacity, location, and utilization to identify idle or overloaded assets and recommend better allocation.

* **Feature 5: Intelligent Recommendations** — Uses AI and supply chain data to provide actionable recommendations for rerouting shipments, responding to disruptions, and reducing operational losses.
